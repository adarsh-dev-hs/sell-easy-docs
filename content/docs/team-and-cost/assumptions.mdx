## How to update the model

<Steps>
<Step>
**Change the input** in the tables above (for example a new salary band, a signed vendor quote or a different start date).
</Step>
<Step>
**Recompute people cost:** for each role, monthly rate = annual fully-loaded cost ÷ 12; multiply by the FTE in each month from [Team plan](/docs/team-and-cost/team-plan#fte-by-month); sum months 1–6 and 1–9. Do this for low, expected and high bands.
</Step>
<Step>
**Recompute non-people lines:** update the monthly ramps for infrastructure, vendors, Claude, tooling and compliance; apply the low and high multipliers.
</Step>
<Step>
**Add up** people + non-people per scenario; apply contingency (10%, 12.5%, 15%).
</Step>
<Step>
**Update the pages** in this order: [People cost](/docs/team-and-cost/people-cost) → [Infrastructure cost](/docs/team-and-cost/infrastructure-cost) → [Vendor & AI cost](/docs/team-and-cost/vendor-and-ai-cost) → [Total budget](/docs/team-and-cost/total-budget) → [overview](/docs/team-and-cost).
</Step>
<Step>
**Record the change** in the change log below with the date and reason.
</Step>
</Steps>

A spreadsheet with one sheet per page (rates, FTE plan, ramps, totals) is the easiest way to keep these in sync; the tables here are its published view.

## Sensitivity

Effect on the **9-month expected total** for the **blended** team ($774k before contingency) when one input changes and everything else stays the same.

| Input change | Effect on 9-month total | Relative impact |
|---|--:|---|
| Move the whole team to US rates (US-heavy scenario) | +$727k | ●●●●● |
| Move the whole team to India rates (India-heavy scenario) | −$318k | ●●●● |
| All salaries at the high end of their bands | +$125k | ●●● |
| Timeline slips by 2 months at full team (steady-state burn) | +$160k – $220k | ●●● |
| Add a US-based senior engineer for 9 months | +$165k – $190k | ●●● |
| Lead engineer and PM hired in India instead of the US | −$255k | ●●●● |
| GTM role deferred to Phase 4 (starts M7) | −$32k | ●● |
| Bombora contract from month 4 ($2k–$5k per month) | +$12k – $30k | ●● |
| SOC 2 work deferred beyond month 9 | −$40k | ●● |
| Claude usage 3× the plan | +$29k | ● |
| AWS cost 2× the plan | +$23k | ● |
| FX moves ±5% (₹81–₹89 per $) | ∓$10k | ● |
| Tooling at $400 per FTE per month instead of $250 | +$12k | ● |

**Takeaways**

1. **Team location and seniority dominate.** Apart from a schedule slip, no other single input moves the total as much as one extra US hire.
2. **Schedule slips are expensive** because burn is mostly fixed people cost. Protect the critical path ([Timeline](/docs/roadmap/timeline#critical-path)).
3. **Vendor and AI costs are small during the build** but become the main variable cost after GA. They should be modelled against revenue per workspace from then on.

Calculation notes: lead + PM to India = 9 months × ($21.7k − $6.3k) + 9 × ($17.5k − $4.6k) ≈ $255k. Two-month slip = 2 × blended steady-state burn ($80k–$110k). Claude 3× = 2 × $14.5k. AWS 2× = $22.6k. FX ±5% applies to India-based people cost in the blended team ($202k).
