This page covers three cost lines: **third-party vendors** used by the product, **Claude (LLM) usage**, and **team tooling**. Infrastructure is on [Infrastructure cost](/docs/team-and-cost/infrastructure-cost).

<Callout type="warn" title="Indicative — verify with each vendor">
Prices below are **indicative ranges** based on publicly listed tiers where they exist. Many of these vendors are **quote-based or change tiers often** (Bombora, Apollo enterprise, Clearbit / HubSpot Breeze Intelligence, RB2B, HeyReach, Instantly, Mailpool, Fireflies). Treat every figure as a starting point for a conversation and **verify with the vendor** before budgeting.
</Callout>

## Who pays for vendors?

SellEasy stores **per-workspace vendor keys** ([Integrations](/docs/product/features/integrations)), so customers can bring their own contracts. That changes who pays:

| Model | SellEasy pays | Customer pays | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bring your own key** (default) | Development, sandbox and pilot-support plans only | Their vendor subscriptions | Enrichment, TAM, intent, scraping, Instantly / Mailpool, HeyReach, Fireflies |
| **OAuth app** | Developer accounts (usually free); marketplace or security-review fees | Their CRM licences | HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio |
| **Platform-owned** | Full usage (cost of goods sold) | Included in the SellEasy plan (with limits) | Anthropic Claude, AWS SES |
| **Resold / bundled** (optional, later) | Wholesale contract | Credits in the SellEasy plan | Possible for enrichment or intent — not assumed in this budget |

The budget assumes **bring your own key** for everything except Claude and SES.

## Claude (LLM) cost model

SellEasy uses Claude through the `LlmProvider` adapter: `claude-opus-5` by default (`ANTHROPIC_MODEL`) and `claude-haiku-4-5` for fast, high-volume tasks (`ANTHROPIC_FAST_MODEL`). The in-app LLM settings also offer `claude-sonnet-5`.

### Price assumptions

| Model | Input $ / million tokens | Output $ / million tokens | Cached input (read) $ / million tokens |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| `claude-opus-5` | $5.00 | $25.00 | ~$0.50 |
| `claude-sonnet-5` | $2.00 | $10.00 | ~$0.20 |
| `claude-haiku-4-5` | $1.00 | $5.00 | ~$0.10 |

<Callout type="warn" title="Assumption — check current Anthropic pricing">
These are first-party API list prices used for planning. Cached-read prices assume ~10% of the input price. Cache writes cost more than normal input, and the Batch API is assumed to halve token prices for non-urgent work. Web search is assumed at **$10 per 1,000 searches**. Confirm all of these on Anthropic's pricing page before budgeting.
</Callout>

### Formula

```text
monthly LLM cost = Σ over tasks of
    calls_per_day × 30 × regeneration_factor
  × ( input_tokens  × input_price
    + output_tokens × output_price
    + cached_tokens × cached_price ) ÷ 1,000,000
  + tool fees (for example web searches × price per search)
```

### Per-task assumptions

| Task | Model | Input tokens | Output tokens | Notes | Cost per call |
|---|---|--:|--:|---|--:|
| Outreach draft | Opus 5 | 3,000 | 800 | System prompt, playbook guidance, account, contact and signal context; output includes adaptive thinking | **$0.035** (no cache) · **$0.026** (2,000 tokens cached) |
| Outreach draft | Sonnet 5 | 3,000 | 800 | Same prompt | $0.014 · $0.010 (cached) |
| Outreach draft | Haiku 4.5 | 3,000 | 800 | Same prompt | $0.007 |
| Reply sentiment + suggestion | Haiku 4.5 | 1,500 | 200 | Per inbound reply | $0.0025 |
| Account research (Phase 3) | Opus 5 | 20,000 | 2,000 | Plus ~5 web searches | $0.20 · $0.125 via Batch API |

Regeneration factor: **1.2** (20% of drafts are regenerated once).

**Worked example (MVP scale, all Opus, no caching):** 500 drafts/day × 30 × 1.2 × $0.035 = **$630**; 50 replies/day × 30 × $0.0025 = **$4**; 50 research runs/day × 30 × $0.20 = **$300**. Total ≈ **$934 per month**.

### Monthly scenarios

| Scenario | Workspaces | Drafts / day | Replies / day | Research / day | All Opus, no optimisation | Optimised (caching + batch research) | Sonnet for drafts (cached) + batch research |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| **Pilot** (Phase 1–2) | 5–8 | 200 | 20 | 0 | ~$255 | ~$190 | ~$75 |
| **MVP** (Phase 2) | ~20 | 500 | 50 | 50 | ~$930 | ~$660 | ~$380 |
| **GA** (Phase 3) | ~100 | 2,500 | 250 | 250 | ~$4,670 | ~$3,300 | ~$1,890 |
| **Growth** (Phase 4+) | ~200 | 5,000 | 500 | 1,000 | ~$12,340 | ~$8,470 | ~$5,660 |

**What this means**

- At growth scale, Claude costs roughly **$28–$62 per workspace per month**, depending on the model mix. Plan pricing and the per-workspace **monthly token budget** (already a setting) should cover it.
- Cost **per approved draft** with Opus at a 70% approval rate is about $0.035 × 1.2 ÷ 0.7 ≈ **$0.06**, within the $0.10 guardrail in [Success metrics](/docs/mvp-scope/success-metrics#guardrails).
- Research is the largest variable cost; cap it per workspace and run bulk research through the Batch API.
