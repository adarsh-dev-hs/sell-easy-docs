## AI-assisted development tooling

This is the line that makes the rest of the plan work. Three engineers delivering the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap) in 13 weeks assumes AI pair programming throughout — the throughput assumption is quantified in [Team plan](/docs/team-and-cost/team-plan#how-ai-assistance-changes-throughput). Treat these seats as **non-negotiable equipment**, like the laptops.

| Item | Per engineer / month | 3 engineers / month | **3 months** |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Claude Code subscription (Max 5× tier, ~$100) | $100 ≈ **₹8,800** | $300 ≈ **₹26,400** | **₹79,200** |
| Anthropic API top-ups — CI agents, batch refactors, doc generation | ~$17 ≈ **₹1,467** | ~$50 ≈ **₹4,400** | **₹13,200** |
| **Expected total** | **≈ ₹10,267** | **≈ ₹30,800** | **≈ ₹92,000** |

| Case | Setup | Per month | **3 months** |
|---|---|--:|--:|
| **Low** | 1 × Max 5× (the SSE) + 2 × Pro (~$20), no API top-ups | ~$140 ≈ ₹12,320 | **₹37,000** |
| **Expected** | 3 × Max 5× + modest API usage | ~$350 ≈ ₹30,800 | **₹92,000** |
| **High** | 3 × Max 20× (~$200) + heavier API usage for agentic batch work | ~$750 ≈ ₹66,000 | **₹1,90,000** |

<Callout type="info" title="The cheapest line in the budget to get wrong in your favour">
₹92,000 of AI tooling sits against ₹11,50,000 of salary — **8% of people cost**. If it lifts throughput by even 10%, it pays for itself roughly three times over. Conversely, downgrading the team to the low case saves ₹55,000 and risks a schedule slip that costs ₹2.2L per fortnight. Do not economise here.
</Callout>

## The product's own LLM cost

SellEasy calls Claude through the `LlmProvider` adapter for outreach drafting, reply sentiment and (later) account research. That usage is **cost of goods sold**, not developer tooling, and it is modelled separately.

### Price assumptions

<Callout type="warn" title="Assumption — verify current Anthropic pricing">
List prices per **million** tokens, converted at ₹88 per USD. Cached reads are assumed at roughly 10% of the input price; the Batch API is assumed to halve token prices for non-urgent work. **Confirm all of these on Anthropic's pricing page before committing to a number.**
</Callout>

| Model | Input $ / Mtok | Output $ / Mtok | **Input ₹ / Mtok** | **Output ₹ / Mtok** |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| `claude-opus-5` | $5.00 | $25.00 | **₹440** | **₹2,200** |
| `claude-sonnet-5` | $2.00 | $10.00 | **₹176** | **₹880** |
| `claude-haiku-4-5` | $1.00 | $5.00 | **₹88** | **₹440** |

### Formula

```text
monthly cost (₹) =
    calls_per_day
  × 30
  × regeneration_factor
  × [ (input_tokens  ÷ 1,000,000) × input_price_inr
    + (output_tokens ÷ 1,000,000) × output_price_inr ]
```

Assumptions used below: an outreach draft is **3,000 input / 800 output tokens** (system prompt, playbook guidance, account, contact and signal context, plus the drafted email); a reply triage is **1,500 / 200**; the **regeneration factor is 1.2**, meaning one draft in five is regenerated once.

### Cost per call

| Task | Model | Tokens (in / out) | Working | **₹ per call** |
|---|---|---|---|--:|
| Outreach draft | Opus 5 | 3,000 / 800 | (0.003 × ₹440) + (0.0008 × ₹2,200) = ₹1.32 + ₹1.76 | **₹3.08** |
| Outreach draft | Sonnet 5 | 3,000 / 800 | (0.003 × ₹176) + (0.0008 × ₹880) = ₹0.53 + ₹0.70 | **₹1.23** |
| Outreach draft | Haiku 4.5 | 3,000 / 800 | (0.003 × ₹88) + (0.0008 × ₹440) = ₹0.26 + ₹0.35 | **₹0.62** |
| Reply sentiment + suggestion | Haiku 4.5 | 1,500 / 200 | (0.0015 × ₹88) + (0.0002 × ₹440) = ₹0.13 + ₹0.09 | **₹0.22** |

### Three usage scenarios

Monthly, 30 days, regeneration factor applied to drafts.

| Scenario | When | Drafts / day | Replies / day | **All Opus** | **All Sonnet** | **All Haiku** |
|---|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| **Development & evaluation** | M1–M2 (Oct–Nov) | ~150 model calls/day from test and eval runs, no live traffic | — | ₹15,000 | **₹6,000** | ₹3,000 |
| **Pilot** | M3 (Dec), 3–5 workspaces | 200 | 20 | **₹22,300** | ₹9,000 | ₹4,600 |
| **Early post-launch** | Jan 2027 onwards, ~15 workspaces | 500 | 50 | ₹55,800 | ₹22,500 | ₹11,500 |

Worked example, the pilot row on Opus: `200 × 30 × 1.2 × ₹3.08 = ₹22,176` for drafts, plus `20 × 30 × ₹0.22 = ₹132` for reply triage, total **₹22,308**.

**What that tells you**

- **Model choice moves this line 5×.** Sonnet drafts cost 40% of Opus; Haiku costs 20%. Run an eval set before defaulting to Opus — if Sonnet clears your approval-rate bar, it saves ₹13,000 a month at pilot scale alone.
- **Prompt caching is the next lever.** Caching the ~2,000-token system and playbook prefix cuts input cost on repeat calls by roughly 90%, which is about 25% off an Opus draft.
- **Cost per approved draft** on Opus at a 70% approval rate is `₹3.08 × 1.2 ÷ 0.7 ≈ ₹5.3` — comfortably inside a ₹9 (≈$0.10) guardrail. Track it against [Success metrics](/docs/mvp-scope/success-metrics).
- **Research is the runaway risk** once it ships. An account research run at 20,000 in / 2,000 out on Opus costs about **₹13 per account**, before web search fees. Cap it per workspace and run it through the Batch API.

## Data, intent, outreach and CRM vendors

### Who pays

SellEasy stores **per-workspace vendor credentials** ([Integrations](/docs/product/features/integrations)), so pilot customers connect their own accounts. That is the central assumption of this line.

| Model | SellEasy pays | Customer pays | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bring your own key** (the default) | Development and sandbox tiers only | Their own vendor subscriptions | Enrichment, TAM, intent, outreach, LinkedIn |
| **OAuth app** | A developer account, normally free | Their CRM licences | HubSpot |
| **Platform-owned** | Full usage as cost of goods sold | Included in the plan | Anthropic Claude, AWS SES |

<Callout type="warn" title="The assumption to challenge first">
This budget assumes **pilot customers bring their own vendor keys**, so SellEasy pays only for dev and sandbox tiers during these three months. If a pilot customer will not — or cannot — bring a key and expects SellEasy to supply enrichment or intent data, this ₹75,000 line can become **₹2L–₹5L+ in a single quarter**, and an enterprise intent contract (Bombora and similar) runs to tens of lakhs a year. Settle this in writing **before** the pilot starts.
</Callout>
