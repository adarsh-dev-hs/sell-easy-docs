This tab sizes **who builds SellEasy** and **what it costs**, for one specific plan: **three engineers, based in India, for three months**, delivering the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap) with heavy AI assistance. Every figure is a planning estimate in **Indian rupees**, not a quote.

<Callout type="warn" title="Estimates, not quotes">
Salaries are stated as **CTC** and vary by city, seniority and market. Cloud and vendor prices change often and many vendors quote privately. Anthropic prices used here are an **assumption — verify current Anthropic pricing** before you commit. USD amounts are converted at an assumed **₹88 per USD**. Every assumption is listed and numbered in [Assumptions](/docs/team-and-cost/assumptions).
</Callout>

## The plan in one paragraph

Three engineers — one Senior Software Engineer acting as tech lead, two Software Engineers — work from **5 October 2026 to 31 December 2026** (13 calendar weeks). The founder acts as product manager. The SSE owns infrastructure. The team tests its own work. AI pair programming (Claude Code and the Anthropic API) is assumed throughout for scaffolding, adapters, tests, migrations and documentation; that assumption is the reason three people in three months is considered feasible at all.

| Senior Software Engineer (tech lead) | 1 | ₹22,00,000 | ₹1,83,333 | ₹5,50,000 |
| Software Engineer | 2 | ₹12,00,000 | ₹2,00,000 | ₹6,00,000 |
| **Total** | **3** | **₹46,00,000 combined** | **₹3,83,333** | **₹11,50,000** |

The arithmetic: ₹22,00,000 ÷ 12 = ₹1,83,333 for the SSE; ₹12,00,000 ÷ 12 = ₹1,00,000 per SE, ×2 = ₹2,00,000; total run rate **₹3,83,333 per month**, or **₹11.5L over three months**.

There is **no separate PM, designer, DevOps engineer or QA engineer**. See [Team plan](/docs/team-and-cost/team-plan) for how that is covered and what it puts at risk.

<Callout type="info" title="Why one-offs look so large">
Laptops (₹3.3L for three machines) and recruitment fees (₹1.5L) are **one-time** costs that land almost entirely in month 1. Over a three-month window they look like 28% of spend; spread over a year they would be roughly 7%. If the engineers bring their own machines, or you hire without an agency, the expected total drops by up to ₹4.8L on its own — the single biggest discretionary lever in this budget.
</Callout>

## Key assumptions

These are the ones worth arguing about before anything else. The complete numbered list is in [Assumptions](/docs/team-and-cost/assumptions).

- **Duration is fixed at three months**, 5 October – 31 December 2026, matching the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap/timeline). Burn is almost entirely fixed people cost, so a slip is expensive: roughly **₹2.2L per fortnight**.
- **CTC is the fully-loaded employer cost.** It covers base, employer PF, gratuity accrual, insurance and bonus. It does **not** cover laptops, software, recruitment fees or workspace — those are budgeted separately.
- **AI assistance is assumed, not optional.** Every engineer has a paid Claude Code seat. The plan assumes roughly a **1.25–1.35× throughput multiplier** overall, not the 5× the marketing suggests. See [how AI assistance changes throughput](/docs/team-and-cost/team-plan#how-ai-assistance-changes-throughput).
- **₹88 per USD** for every cloud and vendor price. A ±5% FX move changes the non-people budget by about ±₹40,000.
- **Customers bring their own vendor keys** for enrichment, intent and outreach tools. SellEasy pays only for development and sandbox tiers during these three months.
- **AWS at list prices, no credits.** An AWS Activate credit would take the ₹70,000 infrastructure line close to zero.
- **No attrition, no notice-period gaps, no raises** inside the three months.
- **Excluded:** founder salary, office, marketing, legal entity setup, penetration testing, SOC 2 and any revenue offset.
