People are about **80% or more** of the budget in every scenario. Figures are **fully loaded annual cost** converted to monthly (÷ 12), multiplied by the FTE plan in [Team plan](/docs/team-and-cost/team-plan).

<Callout type="info" title="What “fully loaded” includes">
**US:** base salary + benefits (health, 401k match), employer payroll taxes, equipment and software, recruiting amortised — roughly **1.25–1.35× base**. Equity is **excluded**.
**India:** CTC (cost to company, incl. PF, gratuity, insurance) + equipment, employer-of-record or entity overhead where relevant — roughly **1.15–1.25× base**, converted at **₹85 = $1**.
Ranges reflect seniority and city (US: Bay Area / NYC at the high end, remote mid-cost cities at the low end; India: Bengaluru / Pune / Hyderabad product companies).
</Callout>

## Contractors vs. full-time

| Option | Typical rate (indicative) | Best for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Full-time employees** | As above | Core roles (lead, backend, frontend, PM) | Hiring lead time (4–10 weeks) |
| **US contractors** | $100–$200 / hour (≈ $17k–$35k / month full-time) | Postgres driver, Salesforce, security reviews | Higher monthly cost; knowledge retention |
| **India contractors / agencies** | $25–$60 / hour (≈ $4k–$10k / month) | Integrations, QA surges, UI work | Quality variance; needs strong review |
| **Fractional DevOps / SRE firm** | $4k–$12k / month retainer | AWS setup, IaC, CI/CD, on-call backup | Define scope and response times |
| **Employer of record (EOR)** | $400–$700 per person per month on top of salary | Hiring in India without an entity | Adds ~5–10% to India costs |
| **Fractional ML / prompt specialist** | $120–$250 / hour | Evals, prompt design, research agent | Keep evaluation data in-house |

**Guidance:** keep architecture, data and security ownership with employees. Use contractors for well-bounded adapters (each vendor is a clear unit of work) and for peaks around the MVP and GA gates.
