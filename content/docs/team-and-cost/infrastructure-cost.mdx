These estimates cover the [target architecture](/docs/architecture/target-architecture) on AWS, with the web app on Vercel or CloudFront + S3. They are **monthly, in USD, for us-east-1 at on-demand prices**, excluding tax and support plans. Check the AWS Pricing Calculator before committing — prices and free tiers change.

<Callout type="info" title="Two sizing points">
**MVP scale** — pilot and early customers: ≤ 20 workspaces, ≤ 100 users, ~50k signals and ~20k emails per month, a few GB of data.
**Growth scale** — after GA: ~200 workspaces, ~2,000 users, ~2M signals and ~1M emails per month, ~100–300 GB of data.
</Callout>

### Vercel vs. CloudFront + S3 for the web app

| Option | Monthly cost (indicative) | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vercel Pro** | $20 per member + usage (bandwidth, function invocations) — typically $40–$200 at these scales | Preview deployments, zero-ops, already used for the demo | Second vendor; usage-based overages |
| **CloudFront + S3** | $10–$60 at MVP, $60–$250 at growth | One cloud, one bill, WAF in front of both web and API | Needs a static-export-compatible build and CI work |

## Cost levers

- **Savings Plans / reserved instances** for Fargate and RDS after GA: typically 20–40% off steady-state compute.
- **Graviton** (`t4g`, `r6g`) instances, already assumed.
- **Scale staging to zero** outside working hours.
- **VPC endpoints** for S3, SQS and Secrets Manager to cut NAT data charges.
- **Log retention** of 14–30 days in CloudWatch; archive to S3.
- **Delay OpenSearch** until Postgres trigram search misses its latency target.
- **Budgets and anomaly alerts** in AWS from day one.
