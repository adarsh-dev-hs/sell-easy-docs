SellEasy serves the whole revenue team. The personas below describe each person's goals, pains, jobs-to-be-done and the screens they use most. Each persona maps to one of the four product roles (**Owner**, **Admin**, **Member**, **Viewer**), which control what they can change. See [Roles & permissions](/docs/product/roles-and-permissions).

The demo dataset uses this same cast: Jordan Lee (Head of Sales, owner), Sam Rivera (RevOps Lead, admin), Taylor Brooks (AE, member), Casey Morgan (SDR, member), Riley Chen (Marketing Ops, viewer) and Alex Kim (SDR, invited).

## Persona → role mapping

| Persona | Typical role | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Head of Sales / CRO | **Owner** (or Admin) | Owns the workspace and plan. Needs full visibility and final say. |
| RevOps | **Admin** | Changes the ICP, playbooks, integrations, mailboxes, team and API keys. |
| SDR | **Member** | Works accounts, approves drafts, runs sequences and handles replies. |
| AE | **Member** | Manages deals and accounts. Books meetings. |
| Marketing Ops / analyst | **Viewer** | Reads signals, analytics and attribution. Changes nothing. |
| Workspace admin / owner | **Owner** / **Admin** | Handles seats, plans, invites, keys and resets. |

## Head of Sales / CRO

**Product role:** Owner (usually the person who created the workspace)

- **Goals:** hit the number, grow pipeline coverage, get reps to spend time on the right accounts, and prove what the agent contributes.
- **Pains:** can't see which accounts are heating up. Forecast relies on rep updates. Hard to tell whether outbound tooling produces anything.
- **Jobs-to-be-done:**
  - "Show me at a glance how much open and weighted pipeline we have and what the agent did today."
  - "Tell me what share of new pipeline the agent sourced."
  - "Show me which reps are winning and which tiers convert."
- **Key screens:** [Dashboard](/docs/product/features/dashboard), [Pipeline](/docs/product/features/pipeline), [Analytics](/docs/product/features/analytics), Agent activity.
- **Owner-only actions:** change plan and seats, reset the workspace (development only).

## RevOps

**Product role:** Admin

- **Goals:** a clean, de-duplicated account base, a trustworthy scoring model, and automation that reflects the sales process.
- **Pains:** duplicate records from many imports, scoring changes with unknown impact, too many vendor tools to manage, and hand-built routing rules.
- **Jobs-to-be-done:**
  - "Import our TAM from a spreadsheet without creating duplicates."
  - "Change the ICP and see how tiers shift before I commit."
  - "Write a playbook: when a Tier A/B account visits pricing, route it, draft an email and alert the owner."
  - "Connect Clearbit and Apollo and set which one we try first."
  - "Create an API key so our warehouse or Zapier can push signals."
- **Key screens:** [ICP & scoring](/docs/product/features/icp-scoring), [Agent → Playbooks](/docs/product/features/orchestration-agent), [Integrations](/docs/product/features/integrations), [Data import](/docs/product/features/data-import), Accounts → Duplicates, [Settings](/docs/product/features/settings) (Team, API keys), Outreach → Mailboxes.

## SDR

**Product role:** Member

- **Goals:** book meetings. Work the hottest accounts first. Send relevant messages quickly.
- **Pains:** static lists, generic templates, researching every account by hand, and missing the moment when an account shows interest.
- **Jobs-to-be-done:**
  - "Show me the Tier A accounts assigned to me, ranked by intent."
  - "Give me a draft that references the signal, let me tweak it and send it."
  - "Put these contacts into the right sequence and skip the ones that bounced."
  - "When someone replies positively, let me reply and book the meeting in one click."
- **Key screens:** [Accounts](/docs/product/features/accounts) (My accounts tab), Agent → Approvals, [Outreach](/docs/product/features/outreach) (Sequences, Inbox), [Contacts](/docs/product/features/contacts), [Signals](/docs/product/features/signals).
- See the [daily SDR workflow](/docs/product/user-journeys#c-daily-sdr-workflow).

## AE (Account Executive)

**Product role:** Member

- **Goals:** move qualified opportunities to close and keep the CRM accurate without double entry.
- **Pains:** meetings arrive without context, deal updates have to be typed into the CRM, and the forecast drifts.
- **Jobs-to-be-done:**
  - "When a meeting is booked, give me a deal with the account's context and signal history."
  - "Let me drag deals across stages and have the probability update."
  - "Push my changes to the CRM."
- **Key screens:** [Pipeline](/docs/product/features/pipeline) (board and deal sheet), account detail (Signals, Deals and Activity tabs), Outreach → Inbox.

## Marketing Ops / analyst

**Product role:** Viewer (read-only; viewers can still use the ICP what-if preview, which saves nothing)

- **Goals:** understand which channels and signals produce pipeline and report on funnel conversion.
- **Pains:** attribution models that credit only the last touch, and data spread across many tools.
- **Jobs-to-be-done:**
  - "Which signal types appear before deals that close?"
  - "What is our source mix and funnel conversion over the last 90 days?"
  - "Export the deals created this quarter as CSV."
- **Key screens:** [Analytics](/docs/product/features/analytics), [Signals](/docs/product/features/signals), [Dashboard](/docs/product/features/dashboard).

## Workspace admin / owner

**Product role:** Owner or Admin

- **Goals:** manage access safely, stay within the seat limit, and keep credentials secure.
- **Jobs-to-be-done:**
  - "Invite a new SDR and share their join link."
  - "Change someone's role or remove a leaver. Their accounts become unassigned and their deals and sequences move to me."
  - "Rotate a vendor API key, or revoke a leaked SellEasy API key."
- **Key screens:** [Settings](/docs/product/features/settings) (Organization, Team, API keys), [Integrations](/docs/product/features/integrations).
